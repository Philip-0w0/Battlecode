Updated Python my-bot; C++ my-bot-cpp remains at its prior validated version.

When a champion carries gems, tactical engagement now counts a healthy ally as
support only if it is not recalling, submitting gems or teleporting, and is
already in attack range or can reach an attack tile in one movement phase.
Movement respects terrain, champions, camps and teleport blockers. The acting
champion stays occupied in allied path checks. A distance prefilter and turn
budget checks bound this extra work. Empty-carrier engagements and bank defence
keep their existing rules. This affects the tactical controllers on Packs/Eight.

Why: the 89 supplied opponent records showed substantial PvP gem losses. Nearby
allies do not necessarily provide immediate support. The earlier blanket ban on
pursuit while carrying gems failed, so that broader rule was not adopted.

Validation: 62 Python tests passed, including 9 new scenarios for the actual
engagement method. The changed bot won 6/8 complete engine-2.0.0 release games on
both starting sides of Packs and Eight against two frozen opponents: 3/4 against
the current policy and 3/4 against an older tactical bot. Eight was 4/4; Packs
was 2/4. These are small local comparison samples, not a ranked win-rate claim.
No caught bot errors or engine faults occurred in these eight games. There were
26 fuel exhaustion events versus 16 for the opponents, so budget pressure still
needs work. The installed source built successfully and completed two 80-round
smokes on Packs and Eight without caught bot errors.

C++ experiments were not promoted. Immutable camp-design caching passed 48 native
scenarios / 1280 differential fixtures / 4722 assertions, and reduced aggregate
fuel exhaustion events from 640 to 491 across 14 comparisons, but won only 5/14
and lost more gems. The support policy combined with that cache won 1/4; isolated
against the unchanged C++ bot it won 2/4 with one caught error. Performance and
strategy changes need independent validation in the smaller native budget.

Eight's local camp handles 0,1,3 were verified as elk, not Monoliths. The available
summary files omit position/health/channel timelines, so they do not establish
that a particular banking route or accidental boss activation caused a death.
